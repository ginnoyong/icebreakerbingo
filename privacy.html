Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Icebreaker Bingo ("the app") is a free, mobile-friendly bingo game for icebreaker activities. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Who this applies to

The app has two kinds of users, with very different data footprints:

  • Hosts sign in with Google to create a set of 24 phrases and start a session.
  • Players join a session via a shared link or QR code — no account or sign-in required.

Information we collect

If you sign in as a host:

  • Your Google account email address and a unique account ID, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • The 24 phrases you write for your bingo board.
  • A record of each session you start (a randomly generated session ID and the time it was created).

If you join a game as a player:

  • No account, email, or personal information is collected or requested.
  • Names you jot down on your board while playing (e.g. who matched a square) stay in your browser's memory for that game only. They are never sent to or stored on any server, and are lost when you close or reload the page.

We do not use cookies or trackers for advertising or analytics. The app doesn't run any analytics scripts.

How we use this information

  • Your Google sign-in identifies your host account so your phrases and sessions are kept separate from other hosts'.
  • Your phrases are read (without requiring sign-in) by anyone who opens one of your session links, so their board can be built.
  • Your email is shown back to you on your own dashboard so you can confirm which account you're signed into.

We do not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising.

Where your information is stored

Host account data (email, account ID, phrases, and sessions) is stored in a Supabase database with row-level security, so a host can only read and write their own phrases and sessions. The app itself is static and hosted on GitHub Pages.

Third-party services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — used to authenticate hosts. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — hosts the database and manages authentication sessions. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the app's static files. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • jsDelivr — a CDN used to load JavaScript libraries (Supabase's client library and a QR code generator).
  • Google Fonts — used to load the app's typefaces.

Data retention & deletion

Your phrases and session records are kept until you delete them or ask us to. Since the app doesn't currently have a self-service "delete my account" option, you can request deletion of your host data at any time — see Contact below.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for general audiences, including use in classrooms and youth groups. Players never provide any personal information. Hosting (which requires a Google sign-in) is not intended for children without appropriate supervision or consent under applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
