How to Use

A quick guide for hosts and players.

For hosts

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Fill in your 24 phrases by hand, or describe your group in the AI box and tap Generate for suggestions — generate again anytime to top up the ones still missing.
  3. Once you have exactly 24, tap Save phrases.
  4. Tap Start Session to get a shareable link and QR code.
  5. Share the link or show the QR code — players join instantly, no sign-in needed.
  6. Tap Start New Session any time for a fresh link. Old links stop working once you do.

For players

  1. Scan the QR code or open the link your host shared.
  2. Mingle and find someone who matches a square on your board.
  3. Tap the square, optionally jot their name, then tap Mark it.
  4. Get five marked squares in a row — across, down, or diagonal — to win. The center square is a free space.
  5. Tap Reshuffle for a new layout, or Clear to wipe your marks and start over.

Tips

  • You need exactly 24 phrases to save — the counter above the textarea shows how many you currently have.
  • AI generation skips phrases you've already got, so you can top up a partial list instead of starting from scratch.
  • Each player's reshuffle only changes their own board — everyone can play independently from the same link.
